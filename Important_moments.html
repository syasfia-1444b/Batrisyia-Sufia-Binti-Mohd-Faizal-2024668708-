Important Moments in My Life

One of my important moments in life is during my first day at university. I feel nervous because everything is new. During orientation, I meet a group of students who share the same interests as me. We slowly become close friends, and together we support each other through classes, projects, and exams. Those friendships make university more enjoyable and help me grow both academically and personally.

One of my important moments in life is during my final year of high school. I work hard on an art project so that I could enter an Art major at UiTM Kedah. After the results came out, I was accepted into the university, but I was offered a different course. Although I felt disappointed at first, I slowly learned to adapt and continue studying. This experience taught me perseverance and resilience.
